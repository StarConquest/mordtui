# Mordtui specification

Status: approved requirements; interaction quality revisions, 2026-10-20.
“Shall” identifies a requirement; “should” identifies a preference. These are
product requirements, not a claim that the current Rust scaffold implements them.
Requirement IDs are stable references for implementation and acceptance checks.

# Purpose and scope

Mordtui is an offline-first CLI/TUI for managing a Mordheim warband throughout a
campaign: roster construction, battle notes, experience and advances (plus-ups),
injuries, recruitment, equipment, treasury, and historical review. It shall replace
the bookkeeping performed in specification/reikland_concept.xlsx while preserving
an auditable record. Optional AI assistance shall use an OpenAI-compatible endpoint.
Users shall define their warband's rules in separate human-readable files; using
a custom warband shall not require writing Rust or using AI.

The first release targets a single local user and multiple local campaigns and
warbands. Shared campaign servers, simultaneous multiplayer editing, a tactical
map, automated combat resolution, and a comprehensive bundled rules library are
outside the first release. Battle mode records tabletop play rather than simulating it.

# Review of the example spreadsheet

The workbook has Summary, Henchmen, Heroes, Ledger, Inventory, Hero_Base_Stats,
Henchmen_Base_Stats, and Sheet8 worksheets. The following observations inform
requirements; spreadsheet values are evidence of workflow, not rules authority.

- Summary names the warband “Burgeoning Burgomeisters,” labels it Marienburg, and
  displays cached values of rating 122, upkeep 0, treasury 2, and wyrdstone 0.
- Heroes separates base profiles from advances. Hans is a Captain with 28 marked
  experience entries, including +W and +BS; two Champions and two Youngbloods
  also have marked advances. An advance marker occupies an experience box.
- Henchmen contains three named Marksmen groups of two models and a Swordsman
  group of one. Group equipment is per model; group quantity multiplies equipment
  consumption and rating. The twelve models include five Heroes and seven Henchmen.
- Ledger records starting funds, hires, purchases, free finds, quantities, unit
  prices, gold changes, dates, and notes. Inventory derives owned, assigned, and
  unassigned quantities from those transactions and the roster.
- The example uses 700 initial gold and five purchased initial plus-ups at 15 gold
  each. Marksmen base BS is 4 despite the Marienburg summary label and the Reikland
  filename. These choices require explicit campaign configuration or reconciliation.
- The Swordsman's ledger hire costs 35 gold; that amount must be preserved as a
  recorded transaction even if a selected rules profile quotes another price.
- Dates include “Initial” and blanks, and Sheet8 contains “1 Search Left” and
  “12 Oct.” These need setup-phase labels, unknown-date handling, and a structured
  search allowance; the year and intended search rule cannot be inferred.
- Fixed roster blocks, text-based item matching, cached formulas, and free-text
  effects shall be replaced by identities, derived totals, and structured effects.
  Import shall preserve notes and flag conflicts rather than silently repair them.

# Tooling and interfaces

TECH-01. Implementation shall use Rust, Ratatui for the TUI, and Rig for optional
AI integration. Core campaign operations shall be shared between CLI and TUI.
TECH-02. Core operation shall require no account, network access, or AI service.
CLI-01. The CLI shall support campaign/warband creation and selection, rules
validation, roster inspection, recruitment/removal, experience/advance recording,
ledger and inventory operations, battle lifecycle, snapshots, history, validation,
and export. Commands shall accept stable IDs as well as unambiguous names.
CLI-02. CLI reads shall support human-readable output and machine-readable JSON.
Mutations shall expose validation failures and exit nonzero on failure; ambiguous
names shall never select an arbitrary entity. Help shall document date, reason,
campaign, and warband selection options. Exact command spelling remains a design choice.
TUI-01. The TUI shall have a summary, roster, warrior/group detail, inventory,
ledger, battle dashboard, post-battle checklist, history/snapshot view and AI
assistant workbench (tab 7). Main navigation shall consistently support 1–7.
TUI-02. All workflows shall be keyboard accessible with visible shortcuts, search,
filtering, contextual help, and recoverable form errors. Meaning shall not depend
only on color. Small terminals shall scroll or show a useful minimum-size message.
TUI-03. Summary shall show roster size, available fighters, Heroes, rating,
treasury, wyrdstone, upkeep obligations, and unresolved tasks with drill-down.
Every tab shall show PRE-BATTLE, BATTLE or POST-BATTLE prominently in its shared
header, including a ready indication for a prepared battle. Phase shall be text,
independent of the selected tab or color.

TUI-04. The default Warband quick-look tab shall present a spreadsheet-style
roster table with unit/group name, warrior type, active member count, M, WS, BS,
S, T, W, I, A, Ld, total XP, and pending advances. Every unit/group shall have
its own characteristic labels directly above its numbers, including after
scrolling; a single distant table header is insufficient. Pending plus-ups shall
have an explicit READY marker and count, plus a summary notice. Heroes shall appear before
Henchman groups. Group XP shall be clearly identified as shared. Equipment per
model shall appear in wider layouts and in the selected unit's detail panel.
The selected dossier shall show individual members and availability, loadouts,
base/current profiles, skills/spells, lasting or conditional effects, and notes.
Random characteristics shall remain readable expressions until resolved.

TUI-05. Routine session operations shall be available through visible actions,
searchable menus, and graphical forms, without requiring command syntax, shell
quoting, copied IDs, or editing data files. The command palette may remain an
optional advanced interface. The required graphical operations are:
- Select/create a campaign or warband and import an opening state.
- Hire individuals/groups, reinforce an existing group, rename members, and
  record death/dismissal/retirement with explicit equipment disposition. Warband
  shall expose D Dismiss directly. The review shall name the departing member,
  explain whether equipment returns to stash or is lost, show any need for a new
  leader, and state that no gold is refunded. Departures shall remove members from
  the active roster while preserving identities, notes and historical records.
- Purchase, find, sell, lose, issue, return, and consume equipment; adjust gold;
  and exchange a user-specified number of wyrdstone shards for agreed total gold.
- Add XP, resolve characteristic/ability or initial advances, promote a member,
  record effects and random stats, manage recovery/capture and assign a leader.
- Prepare/start/end/abandon/complete a battle; record individual wounds,
  conditions, named kill/OOA credits, objectives and model notes; resolve post-battle injuries.
- Record the consequences of physical tabletop rolls directly: advances,
  exploration rewards, injuries and available veteran experience. Manage searches
  and hired-sword upkeep and complete/skip ordered post-battle stages. No normal
  workflow shall require a dice log, roll link, dice ID or in-app roller.
- Add/update notes on a warband, unit, individual or battle; create/inspect/restore
  checkpoints; undo changes; and export rosters, ledgers, inventory and backups.

TUI-06. Pickers shall display meaningful names and context (type, group, prices,
stock or status) while passing stable IDs to the shared engine. They shall be
searchable, retain selections on validation errors, and distinguish duplicate
names. Forms shall default to the currently selected unit, member, equipment,
checkpoint or stage where applicable. Changing a participant shall load that
participant's current battle counters rather than reuse another member's values.
Stage-gated purchase/hiring pickers shall offer the relevant catalog: rare goods
during rare searches, common goods during recruitment, and Dramatis Personae
during their search stage.

TUI-07. Forms shall support keyboard navigation and editing, labeled text and
choice fields, checkboxes, cancellation, a review screen, and a clearly labeled
Apply action. Purchase/hire reviews shall show quantities, actual costs and
treasury impact; equipment reviews shall show copies per model times group size.
Reviews of departure and database-wide undo/restore shall describe their effects.
An invalid submission shall preserve entries and display a recoverable error;
cancellation shall not create an event or consume gold, stock or searches.
Each accepted submission shall apply once through ordinary engine validation.
The first typed input in a prefilled text/numeric field shall replace its default.
Subsequent input and focus changes shall preserve the user's entry. Explicit
Left/Right/Home/End cursor movement shall allow editing the default in place;
Backspace/Delete shall clear an untouched default. This applies to mouse-focused
fields and dynamically loaded values, and shall be explained in the field hint.

TUI-08. The Battle tab shall display one row per participating individual,
including members of a Henchman group, with current wounds, condition, credited
kills and objectives. Wound, objective and out-of-action shortcuts shall save
immediately and preserve unrelated counters and notes. Current wounds shall never
alter permanent W; out of action shall never imply death. There shall be no
separate attribution field: ordinary model notes shall capture observations about
wounds or casualties. Existing attribution text shall remain readable as model
notes in previews and expanded details, without rewriting historical generations.
K shall open a reviewed kill/OOA credit, defaulting to the selected warrior and
allowing a free-text opponent name/description. Blank opponents shall be allowed.
Each credit shall increment the total once and retain its own identity. J shall
let the player select which credit to remove, including unspecified older totals;
zero kills shall report nothing to remove without saving an event. Opponent names
shall appear beside the selected battle state and in expanded details. Credits
shall not automatically award XP or mark an opponent dead. Counter edits shall
not silently discard named entries; the player shall remove those entries first.
The Battle table shall include all nine characteristics in condensed columns,
with Rem (wounds remaining) explicitly separate from characteristic W. V shall
expand the complete current profile, counters, kill log, notes and frozen
pre-battle reference; wider layouts shall show the frozen reference beside the
current state. Bracket keys shall add/remove objective credits. F5 shall consistently
open battle preparation, start or end from any main view; the header shall name
the current action.

TUI-09. The Post-battle tab shall show ordered stages, completion status, the
current stage, unresolved injuries/advances, veteran availability and search
allowances. Its workspace shall include selectable members/groups and relevant
status, XP, loadouts or searches. Forms shall target that selection in place;
players shall not need to switch to the battle roster to apply outcomes. Stage
and target panes shall support both keyboard focus and mouse selection. Scrolling,
filtering and returning from another tab shall preserve the selected stage and
target context. Every pending stage shall expose its own actions and explicit
skip, even before earlier stages finish; completed/skipped stages are read-only.
On entry and after stage completion, the stage list shall receive focus so
Up/Down immediately browses every stage without completing/skipping anything.
Tab/Shift-Tab shall focus targets for row selection. Arrow navigation in an empty
target list shall return to stage browsing, rather than silently doing nothing.

TUI-10. Stash shall use a table of item, type, price, availability, owned, issued
and unassigned quantities, with a named-owner detail view. Journal shall offer
separate ledger, notes, history, checkpoint and injury views. History shall be
the default journal view. F4 shall open a warband-wide injury list from any tab,
including unresolved casualties, dated/battle-linked resolved outcomes (including
descriptive injuries), lasting characteristic effects, recovery and captivity.
The selected warrior dossier shall also identify injury outcomes explicitly.
Standard session screens
shall use labeled tables, summaries and dossiers instead of serialized/debug
representations of the underlying database.

TUI-11. All graphical mutations shall expose effective date and optional notes.
Every mutation shall have a meaningful generated reason describing the action,
named targets and entered consequences. Forms shall update this default as data
changes, preserve a user's replacement, and include it in review, history and
any linked ledger entries. Battle shortcuts and direct stage completion shall
also record clear reasons. Users shall not have to type a reason for routine work.
Ordinary manual outcomes shall not require a reason or a generic house-rule
checkbox. Editable actual prices, shard sale totals and physical-roll consequences
are authoritative player inputs. An exception control may appear only when an
actual profile preset conflicts with the entry, such as a fixed base stat,
characteristic cap, equipment restriction or recruitment limit. It shall name
that specific preset and retain ordinary stock/funding/identity checks. IDs and
serialization syntax shall remain implementation details. Note editing shall
preserve prior versions; restoring history may still require an explanation.

TUI-12. Keyboard and mouse shall both support tabs, rows, action buttons, form
fields and choice pickers. Shortcuts shall be visible in the relevant view.
The layout shall support at least an 80-column by 24-row terminal with scrolling
lists/forms/reviews/details and a useful message below that minimum. Mouse state
and terminal state shall be restored on exit. Keyboard-only use shall remain
complete, including without a mouse or terminal-specific function keys.

TUI-13. Frontend acceptance tests shall exercise real form/key/mouse routing and
headless terminal rendering. Cover representative hiring, purchase/allocation,
XP/advance and battle/post-battle flows, stable-ID selection, duplicate names,
validation/cancellation, context preservation, and narrow layouts. Existing core
invariant and persistence tests shall continue to pass.

TUI-14. Only actions for the selected actionable after-action stage shall have
active shortcuts or appear in its action menu. Buttons, help and key dispatch
shall derive from one binding definition, with no duplicate action keys within
a stage. Global navigation/help keys remain available, including Left/Right to
switch main views from Post-battle. Tab/Shift-Tab changes its focused pane;
unrelated stage keys shall do nothing. Every pending stage shall have C Finish
and K Skip, with these controls visible at 80 columns. Completed/skipped stages
are read-only; once all stages resolve, B Finish Battle remains available from
every stage. Each action shall validate its own prerequisites independently of
the user's browsing position; a fixed sequence shall not hide its controls.
TUI-15. Finish Stage shall validate and advance directly, without a second stage
picker or a redundant experience-confirmation dialog. Completing the experience
stage confirms the manual XP allocation after all owed plus-ups are resolved.
Completing the final rating stage shall finish the battle and save the checkpoint.
Skipping a stage shall remain an explicit, audited action. Physical veteran
availability shall be entered as a number, not inferred from a required dice record.
TUI-16. F3 shall expose campaign rules from any tab. The persistent "Skip Dramatis
Personae" setting shall hide that stage and automatically record its skip for
all warbands in that campaign, in current unfinished and future battles. Disabling
the rule may reopen only automatically skipped steps in unfinished battles;
completed history and unrelated campaigns shall remain unchanged.
TUI-17. Players shall define equipment and skills/spells/prayers graphically,
including name, cost/rarity/category or skill category, descriptive rules and
equipment lists or eligible skill types/units. Equipment creation shall select
multiple named equipment lists using a searchable checkbox picker. Space/click
shall toggle choices without closing it; Enter/Done shall accept the selection;
Esc shall discard picker changes. Filtering shall retain checks for hidden rows,
with Ctrl-A/Ctrl-U selecting/clearing the shown choices. Source CSV editing or
full profile migration shall not be required. Catalog entries shall be audited
extensions of the selected profile;
earlier generations and frozen battle references shall stay unchanged. Duplicate
names within the catalog shall be rejected. Purchase/equipment/skill pickers shall
offer a New entry action that preserves and resumes the parent form. Cancelling
the definition shall save nothing. Saving it creates the catalog entry only;
purchase, equipment issue and learning remain separate reviewed operations.
Warband shall offer S New Skill and L Record Skill; recording a learned skill
shall explicitly say it does not consume a pending advance. XP-earned skills
shall continue through the ordinary advance queue. Resource/eligibility and
duplicate-learning checks shall still apply to newly defined entries.
TUI-18. Notes attached to an individual or their unit shall be visible beside
that model in Battle, in dossiers and post-battle selection details. A dedicated
panel shall show the three most recently recorded notes, newest first, at 80x24.
Long entries may be clipped to one line so all three previews remain visible;
F6 shall expand the full note history. Warband group previews shall include notes
on all group members as well as the unit; individual battle/injury previews shall
exclude other members' personal notes. Superseded note versions shall remain in
history but not displace current notes in the preview. Keyboard selection and
mouse hover over model rows shall refresh the preview without opening forms or
saving changes; hovering under a popup shall not change the underlying selection.
Battle rows shall mark models with notes. F6 shall work from the injury report,
and the report shall also show recent notes for the selected warrior. Every
visible toolbar action shall identify its working keyboard shortcut, including
Enter Details, find/lose, comparison and export.

# Rules definitions and campaign profiles

RULE-01. Rule definitions shall be separate from individual warbands and include
stable IDs, schema version, content version, title, source URL/document/section,
and classification as published, optional, or house rule. A campaign shall pin
its chosen definitions and overrides; changing a source file shall not rewrite history.
RULE-02. User-authored definitions shall describe faction/subfaction, warrior
types and roles, base profiles, starting experience, recruitment cost and limits,
shared equipment lists and type defaults, skill access, spells/prayers,
progression tables, maximum
characteristics, injury effects, and hired-sword conditions. Non-experience-gaining
types shall be representable. Unsupported mechanics may use a documented manual
resolution with a recorded explanation; they shall not be presented as automated.
RULE-03. Campaign profiles shall declare starting funding, faction bonuses,
starting advances or purchased plus-ups, scenario rewards, optional rules, and
house overrides. Derived budgets shall show their components to avoid applying
both a faction bonus and an already-adjusted total twice.
RULE-04. Validation shall identify unknown references, duplicate IDs, invalid
profiles/tables, contradictory limits, and missing automation support with file
and field context. Definitions are data and shall not execute arbitrary code.
RULE-05. Replacements of rule-defined presets shall be visible and attributable.
Structural corruption, negative item quantities, and impossible references shall
be rejected. Actual manual results and prices do not constitute rules exceptions
and retain an optional justification. Explicit preset replacements shall name
the affected constraint in their audit record. Rule changes shall preview affected entities and create
an explicit migration event rather than silently recalculating prior snapshots.
RULE-06. First-release examples shall include a minimal, documented Mercenary
profile and separate Reikland and Marienburg configurations sufficient to validate
the workbook workflow. Complete coverage of every published warband is deferred.

# Roster, profiles, and rating

ROSTER-01. Campaigns, warbands, individuals, henchman groups, and group members
shall have stable IDs. Names may change without breaking history or ownership.
ROSTER-02. Profiles shall record M, WS, BS, S, T, W, I, A, and Ld. Detail views
shall explain base values, advances, persistent injury changes, equipment/skill
modifiers, and temporary battle effects. Current wounds shall be separate from
the permanent W characteristic. Context-dependent effects shall be labeled.
ROSTER-03. Henchman groups shall share progression and loadout while retaining
individual identities for casualties, equipment ownership, promotion, and notes.
Changing group size shall update required equipment quantities atomically.
ROSTER-04. Recruitment shall validate funding, role/type/faction limits, group
capacity, equipment eligibility, and starting experience against the pinned rules.
Experienced replacements shall account for the campaign's veteran availability
and extra cost. Creation and mid-campaign recruitment shall be distinct phases.
ROSTER-05. Death, dismissal, retirement, capture, recovery, missed games, and
leadership succession shall preserve the member's record and resolve associated
equipment and campaign effects explicitly. A casualty shall not automatically
return equipment to the stash. Succession shall use the selected faction rules.
ROSTER-06. Rating shall be derived with an inspectable contribution per member,
including group experience multiplied by membership and special creature or hired
sword contributions. Roster-limit count, income count, battle/rout count, and
rating shall be separate derived measures, since exceptions can differ [R1, R2].

# Experience, advances, abilities, and injuries

PROG-01. Store starting experience, earned experience, and resolved/pending
advances separately. Starting experience shall not generate retroactive advances.
An advance marker shall not add an extra XP on top of its occupied XP entry.
PROG-02. Scenario and underdog awards shall be proposed from battle facts and
pinned tables, showing beneficiaries and reasons. The user shall resolve ambiguous
awards; kills and out-of-action records shall not automatically imply identical XP.
PROG-03. Crossing a threshold shall queue each owed advance exactly once.
Support recorded consequence choices, skill eligibility, characteristic caps,
and final decisions without requiring the raw dice. Profile exceptions shall be
specific to the conflicting preset.
PROG-04. Group advances and promotion to Hero shall preserve member identity,
experience, equipment, and prior increases. Promotion shall enforce available
Hero slots and track the new Hero's skill access and remaining group resolution.
PROG-05. Skills, special abilities, spells, and prayers shall have definitions and
sources, with learned selections and changes stored on the member. Descriptive
abilities shall remain searchable even when no calculation is automated.
PROG-06. Separate in-battle knocked down, stunned, and out-of-action states from
post-battle injury outcomes. Support individual Hero and Henchman injury methods,
multiple lasting injuries, characteristic penalties, missed battles, death,
captivity, recovery, and special outcomes requiring manual follow-up.
PROG-07. Players roll dice physically and enter the resulting consequence.
Advances, promotions, injuries, stat resolutions, exploration and veteran
availability shall not require linked dice records or a reason explaining the
roll. Optional historical dice records shall remain readable for compatibility;
no current TUI task shall depend on creating one. Reopening or resubmitting an
outcome shall not apply it twice.

# Treasury, equipment, trading, and exploration

ECON-01. The ledger shall retain transaction ID, category, item/member references,
quantity, unit price, total gold change, effective date, battle/phase, and optional
notes/reason. Categories shall cover funding, hires, purchases, sales, finds,
losses, upkeep, rewards, wyrdstone exchange, and explicit corrections.
ECON-02. Use integer gold crowns and quantities. Record actual paid amounts
separately from catalog prices. Normal spending shall reject insufficient funds;
any permitted debt shall require an explicit campaign rule.
ECON-03. Inventory shall distinguish total owned, equipped/assigned, and stash
quantities. Acquisition, allocation, transfer, consumption, sale, and loss shall
preserve conservation; one item cannot have two owners. Free starting equipment
shall be recorded explicitly with zero acquisition cost.
ECON-04. Equipment eligibility shall use named equipment lists scoped to a
rules profile. Each warrior type shall have one default list. Each model shall
resolve one list, with an individual assignment taking priority over a group
assignment and then the type default. Models/groups shall be able to return to
inheriting their default. Multiple warrior types and all their inheriting models,
including future recruits, may share a list. An item may belong to multiple lists;
defining one item with multiple selected lists shall be a single atomic action.
Graphical actions shall create lists with multiple types/items, assign lists to
multiple types/groups/models, and edit existing items' list memberships. Warband
G shall open list assignments; Stash G shall edit the selected item's lists.
Dossiers and item details shall name the assigned/containing lists. Shared edits
shall explain effects on other users of the profile, preserve carried equipment,
and apply to future equipment checks. Equipping a group shall check every active
member's effective list, alongside loadout, stock, skill and phase checks. An
explicit nonstandard-loadout control may replace a list restriction. Promotion
shall preserve applicable individual/group access; profile migration shall map
list overrides and their item dependencies or report an unresolved dependency.
Buying a group loadout shall preview quantity times membership and total price.
ECON-05. Trading shall track rarity/search attempts by eligible member, remaining
allowances, modifiers, variable prices, results, and phase restrictions. Cancelling
a purchase shall not silently restore an already-spent search attempt.
The normal rare-item workflow shall give each participating, active Hero not
taken out of action in the last battle one search [R4, Trading: Availability].
Recovering from that battle's injury shall not restore eligibility. Select the
specific item and record found/failed from the physical roll; either result
spends the search. One success permits one purchase of that matching item.
Insufficient funds, cancellation or invalid purchase shall not consume the
available purchase. Scenario-specific search allowances and legacy workbook notes
shall not determine trading searches. Old unspecified search records remain
readable, with each usable search permitting at most one purchase.
ECON-06. Exploration shall record eligible participants, the player-entered
result and pending consequences. Raw dice, modifiers or rerolls may appear in
optional notes but shall never be required. Applying a resolved result
shall create linked resource/events once; manual table resolution is acceptable
where the rules definition lacks automation.
ECON-07. Wyrdstone shall be stock, not immediate gold. Sale shall show shards
sold, retained stock, applicable income bracket/modifiers, and resulting treasury.
The workflow shall enforce the selected profile's sale allowance [R2]. The user
shall enter the actual shard quantity and total gold without an override checkbox
or mandatory explanation; no warband-size price shall be imposed.
A zero-shard, zero-gold sale shall be accepted and recorded as no sale, preserving
stock and treasury while retaining the once-per-battle outcome protection.
ECON-08. Hired Swords shall track eligibility, hire fee, recurring upkeep,
advancement policy, rating contribution, and restrictions. Upkeep due, paid,
waived, or unpaid departure shall be recorded, with its timing defined by the
profile. Dramatis Personae may use the same contract model with distinct rules.

# Campaign and battle lifecycle

BATTLE-01. Battles shall have stable IDs, editable names, effective dates,
scenario, opponents and their pre-battle ratings, result, notes, and lifecycle:
prepared, in progress, post-battle, complete, or abandoned.
BATTLE-02. Preparation shall freeze the participating roster, availability,
loadouts, ratings, and pinned rules in a pre-battle snapshot. Mid-battle reference
shall use that state. Conflicting campaign mutations shall be blocked or explicitly
recorded as exceptions while the battle is active.
BATTLE-03. The dashboard shall allow fast stat/equipment/skill lookup and member
selection, wounds, condition changes, named kill/OOA credits, objectives,
consumables, and notes, including individual members of a henchman group.
BATTLE-04. Rout information should show participating count, casualties, threshold,
leader availability, and applicable exceptions. The player records tests/results;
Mordtui shall not decide tabletop positioning, line of sight, or combat outcomes.
BATTLE-05. Battle changes shall autosave and resume after restart. Abandoning a
battle shall retain its record and require an explicit disposition for consumed
items and pending outcomes rather than silently discarding campaign changes.
BATTLE-06. A resumable checklist shall guide post-battle resolution. For the
reviewed errata profile the ordered stages are injuries, experience, exploration,
wyrdstone sale, veteran availability, rare purchases, Dramatis Personae search,
recruitment/common purchases, equipment allocation, and rating update [R2].
Advances shall resolve within the experience stage. Upkeep and faction-specific
steps shall be placed according to the chosen definitions.
BATTLE-07. Steps shall record pending, in-progress, completed, or explicitly
skipped status. The displayed order is guidance; pending steps may be resolved or
explicitly skipped in a chosen order. Action-specific prerequisites, eligibility,
resource checks and duplicate-outcome protection shall be enforced. The campaign
rule in TUI-16 shall automatically skip the Dramatis Personae stage. Editing a resolved input shall
invalidate affected downstream proposals and require reconciliation; it shall
never silently retain stale income, XP, or inventory results.
BATTLE-08. Completion shall require all mandatory decisions and allocations to
be resolved or explicitly skipped, then create a named post-battle snapshot.
Repeated completion shall not duplicate awards, injuries, sales, or upkeep.

# Persistence, audit, snapshots, and recovery

DATA-01. All authoritative data shall be human-readable UTF-8 text, including
rules, campaign metadata, events, and snapshot metadata. Proposed baseline:
TOML definitions/configuration and JSON Lines events; settle exact schemas before
implementation. Derived caches may be disposable and shall never be authoritative.
DATA-02. Every committed action shall have an immutable event ID, monotonic
sequence, recorded-at timestamp, effective campaign date, optional justification,
related entity IDs, and sufficient structured data for deterministic replay.
Effective date defaults to today and may be supplied by the user. Append order
controls replay; backdating an action shall not reorder dependent transactions.
DATA-03. Corrections/undo shall append compensating or superseding events and
check downstream dependencies. Original actions shall remain inspectable. Changes
spanning treasury, inventory, roster, and history shall commit all-or-nothing.
DATA-04. Users shall create named snapshots at any point and compare roster,
stats, injuries, equipment, XP, and resources between them. Snapshots shall identify
the event position and exact rules versions. Restoring shall create a branch or
an audited restore event without erasing subsequent history.
DATA-05. Writes shall be atomic with safe recovery after interruption, local
single-writer locking, validation before commit, and clear errors on corruption
or conflicting external edits. Unsupported schema versions shall fail safely.
DATA-06. Export and restore shall carry rules dependencies and histories so a
warband can be reconstructed on another machine. Migrations shall preserve a
backup and report changes. Secrets shall not appear in exported campaign files.
DATA-07. Manual file editing shall be supported through validation and an explicit
reconciliation/import path. Inconsistent files shall not silently replace valid
state or bypass the audit record.

# Spreadsheet migration and reports

IMPORT-01. First release shall provide a documented manual migration path and
structured opening-state import; direct XLSX parsing is a later enhancement.
Import shall preview entities, totals, unresolved mappings, and exceptions before
committing a single attributable import transaction.
IMPORT-02. Import shall map all meaningful workbook sheets, preserve original
notes/date labels and cell references where available, and distinguish unknown
values from zero. It shall not fabricate historical battle dates or dice rolls.
Opening balances/progression may be imported without invented historical events.
IMPORT-03. Migration shall reconcile item quantities, member count, experience,
rating, and treasury. Spreadsheet formulas/cached values shall be treated as
comparison evidence; mismatches shall be reported with proposed resolutions.
REPORT-01. Export shall include a portable text/Markdown roster, CSV ledger and
inventory, and JSON state/history. Reports shall identify campaign, effective
snapshot, rules version, and unresolved warnings. Historical reports shall remain
available after members die or leave.

# Optional AI assistance

AI-01. Rig integration shall support configurable endpoint, model, timeouts, and
credentials via environment or a separate secret store. Logs shall redact secrets.
The AI assistant tab, menu action and associated help shall be present only when
the installed program includes AI support. Other installations shall offer six
tabs, ignore the unavailable seventh-tab shortcut and cycle across available tabs.
The in-app workbench shall not present compilation commands or feature flags.
AI-02. AI may explain recorded state, draft names/notes, suggest recruitment or
purchases, and propose structured action plans using supplied rules and resources.
It shall distinguish sourced rules from suggestions and unsupported mechanics.
AI-03. AI actions shall use the same validation and event paths as manual actions.
Before any proposed mutation, show costs, roster/inventory changes, and reasons
for user acceptance. An AI response shall not directly edit authoritative files.
The AI workbench shall expose the full ordinary player command interface, including
battle counters/lifecycle, catalog definitions, equipment, skills, campaign settings,
notes, post-battle actions, checkpoints, imports, exports and history operations.
The provider shall return a structured ordered proposal. It shall have no write
tool and no way to provide or bypass player approval. R shall validate every action
against a disposable copy and show all planned actions, arguments and actual state
changes. Invalid batches, rejection or cancellation shall leave live data intact.
Y shall open a separate approval confirmation; only explicit approval within it
shall publish the reviewed result. Approval shall be single-use and tied to the
reviewed generation; intervening mutations shall invalidate it. Publishing shall
switch CURRENT atomically, retaining ordinary per-action events and AI provenance.
External exports shall remain staged until approval and shall not overwrite files.
AI-04. Network submission shall be opt-in with visible context scope. Service
failures shall leave local work intact; all required workflows shall remain usable
with integration disabled. Accepted proposals shall retain AI provenance in history.
AI-05. Provider requests shall run without blocking the TUI. Context shall cover
the selected warband's roster, pinned catalogs/rules, notes, inventory, ledger,
battle state and checkpoint metadata, excluding unrelated warbands, global event
history, credentials and arbitrary filesystem contents. Requests and proposals
shall support follow-up dialogue. Credentials shall stay in the environment;
no provider shall be contacted until the player sends a request.

# Delivery and acceptance

P0: rules/profile loading and validation; roster and group tracking; XP/advances;
ledger/inventory; battle capture and manual-assisted post-battle flow; durable
history/snapshots; essential CLI/TUI; opening-state import and text/CSV export.
P1: broader automated exploration/trading tables, direct XLSX import, richer
reports, and optional AI. A P0 manual resolution shall still validate resources,
record provenance, and prevent duplicate effects. No rule library breadth is implied.
